[h2]🇨🇳 中文说明[/h2]

[b]写在前面[/b]：
我刚玩没多久，在游玩过程中发现原版长城的逻辑死锁和大运河的历史真实性 Bug，目前只发现并修复了这两个工程问题。
由于个人游玩深度有限，非常欢迎广大玩家共同发掘更多大型工程的 Bug 并提交反馈与代码，一同完善这个 Mod。

[h3]📌 当前已修复内容[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]万里长城（The Great Wall）[/b]
  - [b]修复 AI 发起死锁[/b]：领内无可升级段落（1–3级）时关闭发起条件，杜绝 AI 角色在全满级后反复发起空工程导致永久死锁。
  - [b]修复所有 35 段长城归属判定[/b]：增加作用域多级回退，解决无地产的男爵领的长城段落在 UI 上不显示的问题。
  - [b]解除主建筑师死锁[/b]：取消必须先资助地块的硬性限制，允许随时任命主建筑师。
  - [b]修复朝廷官员发起失效[/b]：修正有效性校验范围为帝国顶级领主，防止工部尚书或中书省摄政发起工程时被系统判定失效作废。
[*] [b]隋唐大运河（The Sui-Tang Grand Canal）[/b]
  - [b]历史路线与水网中枢修正[/b]：纠正原版错用裁弯取直后的大运河，去直取弯，恢复隋唐大运河的真实走向。
  - [b]调整四大渠段 23 个伯爵领[/b]：移出无关的鲁西四州，补齐洛阳、开封及黄河/沁水引水口等关键伯爵领。
[/list]

[h2]🌐 English Description[/h2]

[b]A Note from the Author[/b]:
I started playing CK3 not long ago. During my playthrough, I ran into logic deadlocks with the Great Wall and historical anachronisms with the Grand Canal. Currently, I have only identified and fixed these two issues.
As my playtime is limited, fellow players are warmly welcomed to discover more Great Project bugs, report them, and submit code to improve this mod together.

[h3]📌 Currently Fixed Modules[/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]The Great Wall (`great_wall`)[/b]
  - [b]AI Initiation Deadlock[/b]: Closes initiation triggers when no upgradeable sections (Tiers 1–3) exist, preventing AI from repeatedly launching empty projects after all sections reach Tier 4.
  - [b]Section Ownership for all 35 Wall Sections[/b]: Added multi-tier scope fallbacks, fixing the bug where wall sections in unbuilt baronies (no holding) do not show up on the UI.
  - [b]Master Builder Deadlock[/b]: Removed the strict requirement of having funded a province first, allowing appointment of the Master Builder at any time.
  - [b]Court Minister Project Invalidation[/b]: Corrected validity checks to the empire's top liege, preventing projects from being instantly aborted when initiated by the Minister of Works or Grand Secretariat Regent.
[*] [b]The Sui-Tang Grand Canal (`grand_canals`)[/b]
  - [b]Historical Route & Transport Hubs[/b]: Corrected vanilla's use of the post-straightened canal; restored the authentic curved Sui-Tang canal alignment.
  - [b]23 Counties Across 4 Sections[/b]: Removed 4 irrelevant western Shandong counties; added key counties including Luoyang, Kaifeng, and Yellow/Qin river intake sections.
[/list]
